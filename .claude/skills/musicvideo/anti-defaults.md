# Anti-defaults

Asked for "a music video", an AI reaches first for the same few pictures. They are allowed only as a deliberate
choice that this song earns, never as the starting point:

- a spectrum analyser or waveform bars dancing to the audio
- particles, orbs or a sphere pulsing on the bass / kick
- a neon synthwave grid, a sunset, chrome palm trees, retro 80s gradients
- the lyrics centred on a gradient (or on a blurred blob), one line at a time, in a default sans
- a camera flying through an endless tunnel of shapes
- glitch / RGB-split / VHS noise as a substitute for an idea
- everything reacting to everything: when every element pulses on every beat, nothing lands

Where the concept comes from instead:
- **This song's story** (song-story.md, the lyric's meaning, the user's own notes): who is in it, what happens to
  them, what they want. A video can have a protagonist made of three lines, a place made of type, a dance done by
  shapes. "A lot of dance or some story" means something *moves with intent*: choreography, not reactivity.
- **This song's structure** (song-map.md): its sections are acts; its ★★★ moments are where the rules of the video
  change. Put 3-5 signature moments exactly there (a drop, a stop, a key change, the last chorus, the final hit),
  each with its bar:beat, and make the rest of the video build toward them. Quiet parts get space; the ending lands.
- **This song's sound** (the stems, the notes, the bands): what is *particular* about it: a bass line that walks,
  a voice that holds one note for two bars, a beat that drops out, a tempo change. Map one or two of those to one
  or two visual parameters, on purpose, and leave the rest alone.
- **A rule set**: a small visual language (two or three shapes or marks, a palette, a typographic voice, one kind of
  camera or no camera) committed to for the whole video, with one deliberate rupture.

Before you build, write down the first idea you had and ask whether any song would have produced it. If yes, it is
a default: keep looking. Then look at a style frame of the concept you chose and ask the same question again.
