## What it's about
A single voice talks to itself, or to someone beside it, through a night in a city of rain and lit windows. It counts the lights, admits "nobody knows", and then insists on one thing: *hold the light, never let it go*. The night does not stay steady. It stalls, then quickens (120 → 140 BPM) with "run run run", and the song ends by moving "into the morning". The feeling is tender and a little uncanny: someone small and stubborn carrying a flame through changing weather. The lyric is a transcription from your word-timed LRC. The stems and beats are fallback estimates, so treat the section edges as approximate.

## Lines that could carry a moment
- **"neon rain is falling down"** (0:08.3): the first thing the viewer sees move. Rain starts as the drums enter, and the light bends the streaks around it. The word "down" is held 0.8 s, so let one streak fall slowly for exactly that long.
- **"every window glows"** (0:12.3): windows light one by one on "every / window / glows", with "glows" held as the last window warms.
- **"count the lights and breathe"** (0:16.3): the lights counted are literal, with one small point appearing per word. On "breathe" (held 0.7 s) the whole scene swells and settles once.
- **"nobody knows"** (0:20.3): everything goes still except the carried light. This is the most private line and the picture should be empty around it.
- **"hold the light"** (0:24.3, 0:28.3, 0:32.3): the vow, the same image three times, each slightly closer. "Tonight" (0:30.3, held 0.8 s) is the one added word, so give it its own small change, such as a warmer colour.
- **"never let it go"** (0:36.3): "it" is held 1.0 s, the longest hold in the song. Hold the frame there, with the light tensed and not yet released.
- **"run run run"** (0:42.3): three beats of motion, each one faster than the last, answering the new 140 BPM pulse.
- **"into the morning"** (0:49.1): the first colour that isn't night. Use this line to begin the dawn.

## Where it peaks, where it breathes
- **0:06.3, the intro lift (★★★, +11 LU):** the first sign of life, from nearly silent to full drums two bars later. Use it for the light's first real movement, not for an explosion.
- **0:24.2, the chorus (★★★, +5 LU, high C5):** the song's loudest stretch and its central image, where the light is caught and held. Everything before this builds to it.
- **0:40.3, the tempo change and stop-time (★★★):** the night changes speed, which is exactly your idea. A real hold of 1.5 s, then a faster world. This is where the video's rules change.

## What the user brings
- **"What the song is about to me: a light held through a night that changes speed."** The light is the one constant image and the night is what changes around it. Slow rain and a quiet grid become a held vow in the chorus, a sped-up world in the bridge, and morning at the end. The tempo change at 0:40.3 is the key moment to build the "changes speed" idea on.
- **"Mood / direction: tender, strange."** Tender means a small, warm light, soft edges and no triumph. Strange means the picture's rules can shift without explanation, such as windows lighting out of order, rain bending around the light, or a frozen frame in the stop-time.
